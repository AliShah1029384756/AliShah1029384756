How I Think About Engineering

  • Evidence over hype: claims should be supported by projects, documentation, or clearly stated experience.
  • Fundamentals matter: frameworks are useful, but architecture, algorithms, data, networking, and systems thinking remain the foundation.
  • Maintainability matters: clear structure and documentation make software easier to understand, test, improve, and hand over.
  • Context matters: a team FYP, private system, academic project, and public product should not be presented as if they were the same thing.
  • Teaching improves engineering: explaining a system clearly is often the fastest way to discover gaps in one's own understanding.
