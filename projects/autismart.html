AutiSmart

AI-assisted autism-care and assessment support platform developed as a team Final Year Project at FAST-NUCES.

Team FYP · Public Repository FYP-I: A+ · FYP-II: A-

Why It Matters

AutiSmart was built as a Final Year Project to explore how software engineering, AI-assisted analysis, and healthcare-oriented workflows can be combined into one practical platform. The project was developed, run, and presented by the FYP team at FAST-NUCES.

Project Overview

AutiSmart is a full-stack healthcare-oriented platform designed to support autism-related assessment context, therapy planning, caregiver and expert coordination, progress tracking, reports, AI-assisted recommendations, and therapy-oriented activities. It brings these workflows together through role-aware interfaces and a shared application architecture.

Problem Statement

Autism assessment and therapy support can involve fragmented records, disconnected communication, and manual progress reporting. AutiSmart addresses this workflow problem by bringing assessment support, therapy planning, communication, progress tracking, and reporting into one structured platform.

Core Features

  • AI-Assisted Multimodal Support: audio, video, and text analysis workflows designed to assist assessment and monitoring contexts
  • Emotion Recognition: computer-vision-based signals incorporated into monitoring and therapy context
  • Adaptive Therapy Plans: API-supported recommendations personalized around profile and progress information
  • Interactive Therapy Games: engagement-focused activities designed to support therapy participation
  • Expert-Caregiver Communication: coordinated workflows between professionals and caregivers
  • Progress Tracking: longitudinal activity and symptom information with reporting support
  • Role-Based Security: authenticated workflows with controlled access to sensitive records

Technology Stack

Frontend

React, Vite, responsive role-aware interfaces, and dashboard-style application views.

Backend

Node.js, Express.js, modular APIs, authentication, and application workflows.

Database

MongoDB and Mongoose for structured application and progress data.

AI / Services

AI-assisted multimodal workflows, computer vision integration, dynamic recommendation APIs, JWT, Nodemailer, and Groq API.

Engineering Decisions

  • Role-aware access separates caregiver, expert, and administrative workflows.
  • Assessment, therapy planning, communication, progress tracking, and reporting are organized as maintainable application modules.
  • Longitudinal progress information supports review and iterative therapy planning.
  • The public case study deliberately separates professional proof from sensitive implementation details.

What This Project Demonstrates

  • Full-stack product development in a healthcare-oriented domain
  • Integration of AI-assisted functionality into a real application workflow
  • Role-based architecture and secure application workflows
  • Progress tracking, reporting, and recommendation-oriented product design
  • Collaborative Final Year Project development and presentation at FAST-NUCES
