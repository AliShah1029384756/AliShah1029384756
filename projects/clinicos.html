ClinicOS

Clinic and therapy operations project focused on structured healthcare workflows.

Public RepositoryCase Study

At a Glance

ClinicOS explores how a clinic can bring patient coordination, appointments, therapy sessions, billing, staff workflows, and operational reporting into one structured application.

Problem

Clinic operations can become fragmented when scheduling, patient information, session documentation, billing, and staff coordination are handled through disconnected processes. ClinicOS was designed around the idea of a unified operational workflow.

Core Workflow Areas

  • Patient Management: structured patient information and provider assignment workflows
  • Scheduling: appointment coordination and conflict-aware booking workflows
  • Therapy Sessions: session documentation, notes, and progress-oriented records
  • Billing: billing and invoicing workflows connected to service/session information
  • Staff Coordination: provider availability, specialization, and scheduling workflows
  • Operations Reporting: dashboards and reports intended to support operational review

Technology & Engineering

Frontend

React-based interfaces for workflow-heavy clinic operations.

Backend

Node.js and Express.js for APIs and application business logic.

Data

Relational MySQL data modeling for connected patient, session, staff, scheduling, and billing entities.

Integration

Designed with room for external service integrations where required by the deployment context.

Engineering Focus

  • Separating patient, scheduling, session, billing, and staff concerns into understandable modules
  • Handling workflow constraints such as provider availability and appointment conflicts
  • Designing relational data structures for connected healthcare operations
  • Keeping sensitive implementation details out of the public portfolio

What It Demonstrates

  • Domain-oriented full-stack application design
  • Complex business workflow modeling
  • Relational database design
  • Healthcare-oriented product thinking
  • Privacy-conscious public technical communication
