SchoolIEP

Private education-domain case study focused on structured Individualized Education Program workflows.

Private ProjectCase Study

At a Glance

SchoolIEP explores how an education platform can organize IEP documents, goals, meetings, progress information, and communication between relevant school stakeholders.

Public Safety Note

This page intentionally excludes student records, institution-specific data, credentials, private source code, and other sensitive implementation details.

Problem

IEP workflows can involve multiple stakeholders, documents, meetings, goals, and progress updates. SchoolIEP was designed around a structured digital workflow to reduce fragmentation and make those activities easier to coordinate.

Core Workflow Areas

  • IEP Documents: structured creation, editing, and lifecycle management
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: workflows connecting teachers, coordinators, administrators, support staff, and parents
  • Meeting Management: scheduling, agendas, minutes, and attendance-oriented workflows
  • Goal & Progress Tracking: structured goals and progress information
  • Parent Communication: document sharing, notifications, and progress updates
  • Reporting: organized information for reviewing student-support workflows

Technology & Engineering

Frontend

React-based interfaces for forms, documents, dashboards, and stakeholder workflows.

Backend

Node.js and Express.js for APIs, workflow logic, and document-oriented operations.

Data

Relational MySQL data modeling for connected student, IEP, goal, progress, and audit entities.

Documents

Document-generation and template-oriented workflows for structured IEP outputs.

Engineering Focus

  • Modeling a multi-stakeholder education workflow as clear application modules
  • Managing document and workflow state across an IEP lifecycle
  • Designing structured relational data for connected education records
  • Considering accessibility and clarity for different stakeholder roles

What It Demonstrates

  • Education-domain product thinking
  • Workflow and state-oriented application design
  • Relational data modeling
  • Document-centric web application development
  • Privacy-conscious technical communication
